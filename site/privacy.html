Privacy policy

This website is run by Insource LLC (Anthony Sosa, MA, LPCC) in Denver, Colorado. It explains our services and links to the places where you can book or learn more.

What this website collects

This website has no forms and does not ask for or store any personal or health information. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Booking and third-party services

When you request a session, you are taken to a secure client portal operated for the Neurosculpting Institute. Anything you share there is handled under that portal's and the Institute's privacy practices, and under the confidentiality rules that apply to licensed therapy. Other links lead to Gene Keys, the Neurosculpting Institute, Instagram, Facebook and VoyageDenver, each with its own privacy policy. The website's fonts load from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which receive your IP address as part of normal web traffic.

Messaging

Social media messages are not a secure or confidential channel. Please don't share sensitive health details there; we'll move the conversation to a secure channel.
